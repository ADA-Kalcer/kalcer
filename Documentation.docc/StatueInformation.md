# Statue Information

Discover comprehensive cultural and historical details about Bali's
*patung*.

## Overview

Each statue in Arca's database contains rich information combining
historical facts, cultural significance, and local stories passed down
through generations.

## Information Categories

### Basic Details
Every *patung* entry includes:
- **Name**: Traditional and common names
- **Location**: Precise geographic coordinates and address
- **Physical Description**: Dimensions, materials, and artistic style
- **Construction**: Year built and sculptor

### Cultural Significance
Understanding the deeper meaning:
- **Religious Context**: Role in Hindu-Balinese ceremonies
- **Artistic Style**: Sculptural techniques and influences
- **Local Legends**: Stories and myths associated with the statue

### Categories

#### Ritual Statues
Sacred *patung* used in religious ceremonies:
- Active use in temple rituals
- Specific ceremonial protocols
- Seasonal and festival significance
- Proper etiquette for visitors

#### Monumental Statues
Historical and cultural landmarks:
- Commemorative purposes
- Artistic and architectural significance
- Public access and viewing
- Educational value

## Respectful Engagement

### Sacred Sites
When visiting ritual statues:
- Observe ongoing ceremonies from respectful distances
- Follow local dress codes and behavior guidelines
- Ask permission before photography
- Understand the spiritual significance

### Learning Opportunities
- Read cultural context before visiting
- Engage with local guides when available
- Participate in educational programs
- Support cultural preservation efforts
