# Bookmarking

Save and organize your favorite *patung* discoveries for easy access and
future visits.

## Overview

The bookmarking system lets you create personal collections of statues
that hold special meaning, interest, or emotional significance for you.

## Creating Bookmarks

### From Statue Details
1. Open any statue's detail page
2. Tap the bookmark icon (⭐)
3. The statue is saved to your collection
4. Icon changes to indicate bookmarked status

## Managing Your Collection

### Viewing Bookmarks
Access your saved statues:
- Tap the bookmarks tab in the main navigation
- View as list or map layout

## Advanced Features

### Smart Collections
Automatically generated collections:
- **Recently Discovered**: Statues bookmarked in the last week
- **Nearby Favorites**: Bookmarked statues close to current location


## Cultural Considerations

### Respectful Bookmarking
When saving sacred sites:
- Understand the spiritual significance
- Note appropriate visiting times and protocols
- Include cultural context in personal notes
- Respect photography restrictions

### Community Contributions
- Rate and review statues (cultural sensitivity guidelines apply)
- Contribute accurate information corrections
- Share respectful photography and experiences
- Help improve cultural accuracy
