# Audio Tour

Experience hands-free guided tours of Bali's statue heritage.

## Overview

The Audio Tour feature provides automatic narration about *patung* as you
encounter them, creating an immersive cultural exploration experience.

![Audio Tour in Action](audio-tour-feature)

## How It Works

### Activation
1. Tap the audio tour button on the main screen
2. Grant microphone access if prompted (for optimal audio mixing)
3. Start your journey - walking, driving, or biking

### Automatic Narration
As you approach statue locations:
- Arca detects your proximity using GPS
- Audio automatically begins playing information
- Content includes history, cultural significance, and stories

### Music Compatibility
The audio tour is designed to work alongside your music:
- Music volume automatically lowers during narration
- Seamless transitions between music and tour audio
- Resume music automatically after each segment

## Features

### Background Operation
- Continues working when app is in background
- Works with screen locked to save battery
- Receives location updates for accurate proximity detection

### Customization
- Adjust narration volume independently from music
- Choose between brief or detailed information levels

## Best Practices

### For Walking Tours
- Use headphones for better audio quality
- Keep phone in pocket - audio continues automatically
- Plan routes with multiple statue locations

### For Driving Tours
- Use car's Bluetooth audio system
- Set longer proximity ranges for highway speeds
- Focus on roadside monuments for safety
