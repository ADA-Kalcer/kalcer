# ``Arca``

The self-guided app for statues information spread throughout Bali

## Overview

Bali is rich in culture and one of them is *patung* (English: statue). Arca provides comprehensive statue information spread throughout Bali, allowing you to explore the way you want - super simple and convenient!

![Arca App Hero Image](hero-image)

Discover Bali's cultural heritage through an interactive, audio-guided experience that brings the stories of ancient statues to life. Whether you're a tourist exploring for the first time or a local wanting to learn more about your cultural heritage, Arca makes statue discovery accessible and engaging.

## Key Features

### Self-Guided Audio Tour
Arca provides an interactive audio-based tour guide for *patung* nearby. With just one click, tour mode is activated for you. The audio tour even works while you listen to music - Arca speaks the information about the *patung* you pass on the road.

![Audio Tour Feature](audio-tour-screenshot)

### Detailed Patung Information
Curious about the story and history behind a *patung*? Arca provides comprehensive statue information including:
- Name and cultural significance
- Physical dimensions and materials
- Construction year and historical context
- Sculptor information and artistic details
- Stories and legends associated with each statue

![Statue Details View](statue-details-screenshot)

### Awareness Pocket Guide
*Patung* in Bali are not just monuments - they are integral parts of Balinese rituals and spiritual life. Arca categorizes statues into:
- **Ritual Category**: Sacred statues used in ceremonies and religious practices
- **Monumental Category**: Historical and cultural landmarks

![Category Guide](category-guide-screenshot)

### Bookmark Your Patung
Save statues that resonate with you or hold emotional significance. Create your personal collection for easy access and future visits.

![Bookmarks Feature](bookmarks-screenshot)

## Cultural Context

Arca respects and celebrates Balinese culture by providing accurate, culturally-sensitive information about each *patung*. The app serves as a bridge between traditional knowledge and modern technology, making cultural heritage accessible to both locals and visitors.

> *Patung* in Balinese culture represent more than artistic expression - they embody spiritual beliefs, historical narratives, and community identity.
