# Getting Started with Arca

Welcome to Arca, your guide to exploring Bali's statue heritage.

## Overview

Arca helps you discover and learn about *patung* (statues) throughout Bali
with interactive audio tours and detailed cultural information.

![Getting Started with Arca](getting-started-hero)

## First Steps

### 1. Download and Install
Get Arca from the App Store and install it on your iOS device.

### 2. Grant Location Permission
When prompted, allow location access so Arca can discover nearby statues
and provide proximity-based information.

### 3. Start Exploring
Open the app to see a map with statue markers. Each marker represents a
*patung* with cultural and historical significance.

## Quick Tour

### Finding Statues
The main screen shows a map of your current area with statue markers. Tap
any marker to view basic information about that *patung*.

### Your First Audio Tour
1. Tap the audio tour button
2. Start walking or driving
3. Arca will automatically speak information about statues as you approach
them

### Saving Your Favorites
Tap the bookmark icon on any statue to save it to your personal collection
for easy access later.
